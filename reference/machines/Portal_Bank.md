A Bank Portal converts coins: set the coin it puts out and its **Conversion Amount**, 1 to 50
(1 in a new portal), the coins in each entry it puts on its belt.

It takes coins of any kind, adding up their value, and takes more only while it holds less than
one output entry's worth. Whenever it holds enough, it puts out one entry and keeps the rest, so
nothing is lost. It takes no time, heat or Factory Efficiency: its belts are its only limit, one
entry in and one out per belt slot.

Coins travel 50 to an entry, so the smaller coin always sets the pace: at base Logistics
Efficiency, 60 entries of 50 copper a minute, 3,000 copper's worth, whichever way it converts.
Turning that copper into silver gives 3 silver a minute; turning silver into copper puts out at
most 3,000 copper a minute.

A smaller Conversion Amount puts out smaller stacks: useful for feeding something that should
take coins a few at a time.
