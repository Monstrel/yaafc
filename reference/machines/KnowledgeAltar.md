A Knowledge Altar breaks down the items fed to it for EXP toward your Knowledge Level. It has one
belt input and no output.

Most items give a little EXP each, more for more valuable items, and take longer the more
they're worth: each item's page shows its EXP and time. Factory Efficiency speeds it up.

The planets are **relics**, worth far more. The altar works on a fifth of a planet at a time, so
one planet takes five cycles; Relic Knowledge raises their EXP, and only theirs. At base
upgrades:

- [Jupiter](item:Jupiter): 10 EXP a planet, 1 minute
- [Saturn](item:Saturn): 71.5 EXP, 1.5 minutes
- [Mars](item:Mars): 126 EXP, 2 minutes
- [Venus](item:Venus): 510 EXP, 2.5 minutes
- [Mercury](item:MercuryP): 2,693 EXP, 3.75 minutes
- [Luna](item:Luna): 9,537.5 EXP, 5 minutes
- [Sol](item:Sol): 24,439 EXP, 10 minutes
