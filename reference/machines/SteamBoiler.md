A Steam Boiler turns heat from the furnace under it into [Steam](item:Steam), which it sends
through pipes to [Steam Heating Pads](machine:SteamHeater). Its setting sets its pace:

- **Low**: 30 Steam every 6 seconds, burning 100 P a second.
- **Medium**: 100 Steam every 4 seconds, 500 P a second.
- **High**: 300 Steam every 2 seconds, 3,000 P a second.

Each Steam carries 20 P of heat, whatever the setting, and a heating pad gives all 20 back.
Factory Efficiency speeds it up, making Steam faster and burning heat faster in step.

Pipes carry any amount, so a boiler never waits for its Steam to move on.
