A Paradox Crucible turns any item that travels on a belt into
[Oblivion Essence](item:Mors): one belt entry in, one Oblivion Essence out.

How long an entry takes depends on its value: **1,500 ÷ value seconds**, never less than 0.5 s or
more than 1,500 s. The value is the item's cauldron value, shown on its page; a stack of coins
counts as one entry, worth the whole stack. So anything worth 3,000 or more takes the fastest
0.5 s, and cheaper items take longer.

Factory Efficiency speeds it up, but the time in its panel in game is always the base time.
While it works it burns 1,200 P of heat a second (more with Factory Efficiency, in step with its
speed).

It holds one entry at a time, and time left over from one entry carries to the next, so nothing
is wasted. At its fastest it could make 120 a minute, more than one output belt carries: there,
its output belt sets the pace.
