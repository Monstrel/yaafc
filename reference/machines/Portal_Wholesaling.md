A Dispatch Portal sells goods in bulk on a purchase contract: it takes only its contract's item,
from one belt, and puts the payment out as coins.

## Daily limits

Once it holds a whole contract's worth, it completes the contract if today's total of that item
is still under the day's limit. The total counts **every Dispatch Portal in the factory**, so
more portals don't raise the limit. A contract that takes the total past the limit still
completes; after that the portal stops taking the item until the next day, and its belt backs up.

## Supplier Licenses

Each Supplier License raises every contract's daily limit by 60% of its base, and raises pay:
+8% for the first, up to +60% with all five. Each license also unlocks the next contract; the
first comes from the Sample Request quest. Pay is rounded down coin by coin, so a small bonus can
add nothing: 12 silver × 1.08 is still 12 silver.

## The day

A game minute passes every real second, and factories don't run while you sleep. You go to bed
at the latest at 30:00 minus your Sleep Time, so a day is 16 real minutes at the start, two
minutes longer with each Potion Belt level, up to 24. Bed is allowed from 12:00, but every
minute slept is a minute of production lost.
