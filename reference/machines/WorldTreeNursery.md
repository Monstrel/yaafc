A World Tree grows on its own through three stages, which the player doesn't choose. How fast it
uses nutrients is set by its stage, not by the fertilizer fed to it: fertilizer only fills its
store of nutrients, each one adding its nutrient value (raised by Fertilizer Efficiency). Factory
Efficiency speeds up every stage.

## Stages

1. **Sapling**: its first 6,000,000 nutrients, at 5,000 nutrients/s, make nothing. That's 20
   minutes at base speed.
2. **Young tree**: the next 6,000,000, at 10,000 nutrients/s, grow
   [World Tree Leaves](item:WorldTreeLeaf) at 30,000 nutrients each: 200 leaves over 10 minutes.
3. **Mature tree**, from then on: 20,000 nutrients/s, and every item it grows costs 60,000
   nutrients, twice a young tree's. It grows 100 leaves, then one
   [World Tree Core](item:WorldTreeCore), and repeats.

So a mature tree grows an item every 3 seconds at base speed: about 19.8 leaves and 0.2 cores a
minute. It never outpaces its output belt, which takes one item per slot.

## Feeding it

A mature tree uses 1,200,000 nutrients a minute at base speed. That's 50
[Fertile Catalysts](item:Catalyst2) or 6
[Panacea Potions](item:PanaceaElixir) a minute before Fertilizer Efficiency; any fertilizer
works, and its nine belt inputs can take several kinds.

The Planner plans a mature tree: it doesn't count the 30 minutes a new one takes to get there.
