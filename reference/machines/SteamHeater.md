A Steam Heating Pad heats the machine on it with [Steam](item:Steam) from pipes instead of
burning fuel: each Steam gives 20 P of heat. Fuel Efficiency doesn't apply here.

The Steam comes from [Steam Boilers](machine:SteamBoiler), which put exactly 20 P into each
Steam, so a pad gives back all the heat a boiler took. Fuel Efficiency counts once, when the fuel
under the boiler burns.
