A Thermal Extractor works like an [Extractor](machine:Extractor), with more output the higher it's
built. Its output is multiplied by **1 + height ÷ 128**: ×1 on the ground, ×2 at height 128, and
at most ×3, from height 256 up. "Height" in its panel in game is that height, in grid spaces.

Alchemy Skill raises its output on top of that, as it does an Extractor's. It burns 80 P of heat
a second while it works.
